Reporting Tips

  • Be specific: Mention nearby landmarks so authorities can find the exact spot.
  • Add a photo: Reports with photos get resolved faster.
  • Check the map first: Someone may have already reported the same issue nearby.
  • Rate severity honestly: High-severity reports are prioritized for safety-critical hazards.

Your report helps build a safer road network for everyone in your community.

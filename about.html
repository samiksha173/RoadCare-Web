About RoadCare

Welcome to RoadCare, your trusted partner in revolutionizing road safety and infrastructure maintenance. We connect everyday citizens with local authorities so that road hazards get fixed faster, not forgotten.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower communities to report potholes, broken signals, and road hazards instantly — turning every commuter into a contributor to safer streets.

Our Vision

A future with zero accidents caused by poorly maintained road infrastructure, built through transparency between citizens and local authorities.

Key Features

The tools that make reporting and resolving road issues simple for everyone.

1

Report & Track

Real-time logging of road hazards with geotagging and photos, so nothing gets lost or forgotten.

2

Smart Dispatch

Direct routing of issues to the relevant local maintenance authority — no chasing the right department.

3

Community Awareness

An interactive map view lets citizens check reported issues in their own area before filing a duplicate.
